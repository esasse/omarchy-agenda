What you get

A plugin you install on your own computer, which reads your Google calendars with your own credentials and displays them in your status bar. There is no hosted service, no account to create and nothing to pay.

Your credentials, your project

The plugin ships with no Google credentials. You create an OAuth client in your own Google Cloud project and authorize your own accounts, so the API quota, the consent screen and the data all belong to you. Keep the client secret and the tokens under ~/.local/share/omarchy-agenda/ to yourself; they grant read access to your calendars.
